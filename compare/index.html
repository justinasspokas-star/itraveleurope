1 · Medical protection

Emergency medical + evacuation

  • Confirm the policy accepts residents of your home country.
  • Compare deductible and whether claims are direct-paid or reimbursed.
  • Check who authorizes evacuation and the transport destination.
  • Read pre-existing-condition exclusions and waiver timing.
2 · Protect prepaid costs

Cancellation + interruption

  • Include only eligible non-refundable trip cost.
  • Read covered reasons and purchase deadlines.
  • Check how credits, refunds and unused bookings affect a claim.
3 · Visa applications

Certificate + compliance

  • Use this section when your application requires Schengen medical insurance.
  • Verify at least €30,000, repatriation and required territorial validity.
  • Match traveler name and covered dates to the application.

Before you choose a policy

Questions to ask before buying

  1. Can this insurer sell the policy to residents of my country or state?
  2. What health coverage do I already have abroad?
  3. Would I have to pay a hospital first and claim later?
  4. Who decides whether a medical evacuation is necessary?
  5. How much prepaid money would I genuinely lose if I cancelled?
  6. Which cancellation reasons are covered?
  7. Does the policy have a deadline for a pre-existing-condition waiver?
  8. How long must a delay last before benefits begin?
  9. Are trains and ferries covered under missed-connection wording?
  10. What is the maximum payment for one phone, laptop or camera?
  11. Are my planned activities included?
  12. Does the policy territory include every country on my itinerary?
  13. Is there a maximum trip duration?
  14. What documents would I need for a claim?
  15. What emergency number should I call before arranging expensive treatment or transport?

How to compare price fairly

Two policies are not directly comparable simply because one premium is lower. A cheaper policy may have a higher deductible, lower medical or cancellation limits, narrower delay triggers, weaker pre-existing-condition wording or lower baggage sublimits. Compare the benefit that matters to your trip first, then compare price among policies that meet that need.

Three examples

Five-day city break

A traveler with refundable flights and hotels may place less value on cancellation cover and more on medical, delay and baggage protection.

€8,000 cruise and rail trip

High non-refundable trip cost, missed connections and interruption benefits become much more important.

Traveler with a medical condition

Pre-existing-condition definitions, stability rules, assistance access and purchase timing can matter more than the headline medical maximum.

Visa insurance is a separate check

If you need a Schengen visa, the insurance document also has to satisfy the visa requirement. That is different from deciding whether a policy offers strong cancellation or baggage benefits. See our Schengen visa insurance requirements page.
