Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

How iTravelEurope can earn revenue while keeping provider information, price estimates and comparison criteria transparent.

How commissions work

Some links from iTravelEurope to insurance providers are affiliate links. If a visitor follows one and completes an eligible purchase, iTravelEurope may receive a commission from the provider or affiliate network. The purchase itself is completed with the provider.

What commissions do not decide

A commission does not guarantee a favorable review, a “best” label or a higher editorial ranking. Our comparison methodology is described on the About & Editorial Standards page. Where the results page offers “Best deal” and “Cheapest” sorting, “Best deal” is an editorial default order and “Cheapest” uses the estimated visible trip totals; neither is a market-wide award.

Estimated prices and live quotes

Some prices displayed on iTravelEurope are editorial estimates based on the price assumptions shown on the comparison page. Currency conversions are approximate. The provider's live quote, eligibility decision, policy wording and final price always prevail.

Sponsored links

Commercial provider links are disclosed to users and use sponsored link attributes where appropriate. A commercial relationship does not mean that iTravelEurope compares every product sold by that provider or every insurer available in the market.

Current comparison partners

The comparison may currently show commercial links to providers such as Chapka and IATI when the route or traveler is relevant to the product shown. Availability can change by residence, destination, age, dates and product eligibility.
