City breaks, countryside and winter sports

Travel insurance for France from the USA

A weekend in Paris and a ski week in the Alps carry very different risks. Choose for the activities and reservations you actually have—not for the country name alone.

Compare for France

What to compare for France

Activity coverage

Standard policies may exclude skiing, off-piste activity, cycling events or other higher-risk plans unless an option is added.

Medical and evacuation

Mountain rescue and transport can sit under separate terms from ordinary emergency treatment.

Travel delay

Compare waiting periods, daily caps and which expenses qualify during a covered transport delay.

Rental-car protection

Check whether rental damage is included, optional or excluded—and compare it with the rental company contract.

What makes this trip different

France’s public system does not mean treatment is automatically free for U.S. tourists. Ask how the policy handles upfront payments and reimbursement.

Strikes and announced disruptions can be foreseeable events. A policy bought after a disruption becomes known may not cover it.

If the trip includes the Alps or Pyrenees, define the exact activities before comparing plans; rescue and sport exclusions can matter more than the medical headline limit.

Match coverage to the itinerary

Paris city break

Focus on cancellation, belongings, delay and a sensible medical limit for a short, reservation-heavy trip.

Provence road trip

Review rental-car, roadside and interruption logistics, especially when lodging is prepaid across several towns.

French Alps

Select a plan that explicitly fits skiing or hiking activities and the elevations you expect.

A quick reality check

Travel insurance only covers events, costs and circumstances defined by the policy. Buy for the wording you need, disclose information accurately and keep documentation for every significant booking.

Frequently asked questions

Is a European health card available to Americans?

The EHIC/GHIC systems are not general travel-health cards for U.S. residents. Americans need to check their own plan or buy separate coverage.

Will insurance cover a transport strike?

It depends on the policy, timing and exact covered reason. Known events and schedule changes without a qualifying loss are commonly restricted.

Do I need winter-sports coverage?

If skiing, snowboarding or similar activities are part of the trip, ordinary coverage may be insufficient. Read activity definitions and exclusions.
