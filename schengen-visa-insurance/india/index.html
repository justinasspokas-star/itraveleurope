The core Schengen insurance rule

Article 15 of the EU Visa Code requires applicants for a uniform visa to prove that they have adequate and valid travel medical insurance. The insurance must cover expenses connected with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention, emergency hospital treatment or death.

The same article says the insurance must be valid throughout the territory of the relevant Member States, cover the entire intended stay or transit and provide at least €30,000 of coverage.

Spain visa applications from India: a concrete example

The Embassy of Spain in New Delhi states that travel medical insurance must cover repatriation for medical reasons or death, urgent healthcare and emergency hospital treatment for the entire stay and throughout the Schengen area, with at least €30,000 of coverage. That is a useful example of how the Article 15 rule appears in a real consular checklist.

Do not assume every consulate presents the documents in exactly the same format. Always use the current checklist for the country and visa center handling your appointment.

Before you buy: four practical questions

  • Can the insurer issue a visa certificate immediately? You want a document you can actually submit.
  • What happens if the visa is refused? Read cancellation, refund and administrative-fee rules before paying.
  • Does the policy cover only medical visa requirements or the wider trip too? Baggage and cancellation may be separate benefits.
  • Are the travel dates final? Changing dates after issue may require an endorsement or a new certificate.
Do not buy from the headline alone. “Schengen insurance” in an advertisement is not proof that the certificate matches your application. Compare the actual certificate wording and your consulate checklist.

How to choose Schengen travel insurance from India

A low price is not proof that your Schengen visa insurance will be accepted. Confirm the policy covers an applicant residing in India, then compare it with the checklist issued by the consulate handling your application.

  1. Identify the correct consulate: for several destinations, follow the European Commission's main-destination rules.
  2. Verify eligible insurers: check any local consular provider list and the exact policy.
  3. Confirm the minimum: €30,000 medical cover and the emergency treatment and repatriation protections required by Article 15.
  4. Inspect the certificate: insured name, travel dates, medical limit, Schengen validity and insurer details.
  5. Check alterations and refund rules: read what happens if travel dates change or your visa is refused.

Which Indian travel insurers are accepted for a Schengen visa?

Acceptance depends on your application consulate. As a concrete example, German missions in India say Indian travel medical insurance is accepted only from insurers on their approved list. That is not a blanket guarantee for every policy or for applications to other countries.

Schengen visa travel insurance price in India: what matters?

There is no universal price. The premium can change with age, trip duration, coverage limits, optional baggage and cancellation benefits, medical history and insurer eligibility. Compare real quotes only after confirming the policy meets the visa rules. Example: India to France and Spain on one trip

For an itinerary Delhi → Paris → Madrid → Delhi, check that the insurance covers the entire required period and the appropriate Schengen territory, not just France. Determine which consulate handles your application using the official main-destination rules; the first country entered is not always the correct one to apply to.

Keep your insurer-issued certificate, policy number, assistance contact and the documents requested for your visa appointment. If you need protection for the holiday itself, compare wider benefits beyond the minimum.

  • The insured name matches the passport.

  • The full required period and relevant Schengen territory are stated.

Frequently asked questions

How much Schengen travel medical insurance is required?

The minimum coverage under Article 15 is €30,000.

Must it cover the whole Schengen Area?

The Visa Code requires validity throughout the territory of the relevant Member States and for the entire intended stay or transit.

Should I buy before the visa decision?

Proof of insurance is part of the visa documentation, so applicants commonly need it before a decision. Check the policy's refund or cancellation terms in case the visa is refused.

Is the cheapest compliant policy automatically the best choice?

No. Visa compliance is one requirement. For the trip itself, compare medical limits above the minimum, assistance, exclusions, baggage and cancellation according to your needs.

Must German visa applicants in India use an approved insurer?

German missions in India state that they accept Indian travel medical insurance only from providers on their published approved list. Confirm the current list and the policy.

Is the cheapest Schengen insurance from India enough?

Not necessarily. The policy must meet the consulate's requirements for insurer eligibility, medical cover, repatriation, travel dates and certificate wording.

What if your Schengen visa is rejected?

A travel insurance premium may be refundable under some insurers' visa-refusal cancellation terms, but this is not automatic. Read our Schengen visa rejection and travel insurance refund guide before buying so you know what evidence and deadlines could apply.
