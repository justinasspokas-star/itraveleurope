Travel insurance for Italy from the USA

Italy trips often combine expensive reservations with separate rail, ferry and tour bookings. That makes the definition of a covered delay or cancellation more important than a low headline price.

A quick reality check

Travel insurance only covers events, costs and circumstances defined by the policy. Buy for the wording you need, disclose information accurately and keep documentation for every significant booking.

Frequently asked questions

Do U.S. health plans work in Italy?

Coverage varies. Many domestic plans have limited international networks or require reimbursement claims, so obtain a written answer from your insurer.

Does a policy cover a missed high-speed train?

Only when the reason and transport type meet the policy wording. A short self-planned connection is not automatically covered.

What trip cost should I enter?

Use the amount you would lose if you cancelled on the purchase date: non-refundable flights, lodging, tours and other eligible prepaid costs.
