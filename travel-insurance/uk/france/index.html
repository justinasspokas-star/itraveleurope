Quick answer: do I need travel insurance for France from the UK?

UK travellers should consider travel insurance for France even with a valid UK GHIC or eligible EHIC. The card can help with medically necessary state healthcare on French terms; it does not replace insurance for medical repatriation, private treatment, cancellation or luggage. Compare cover for your journey, not just the destination.

What does GHIC cover in France—and what does it not?

France accepts a valid UK GHIC or UK EHIC for medically necessary state healthcare during a temporary visit. Treatment is on the same basis as for French residents, which can mean charges or patient contributions. Keep the card with you and check that the facility is part of the public system.

GHIC is not a travel insurance policy. It does not pay to fly you home for medical reasons and does not generally cover private clinics, pre-booked cancellations or lost belongings. For an emergency, contact the local emergency services first and then your insurer's assistance team.

How much does travel insurance from the UK to France cost?

There is no trustworthy fixed price for every traveller. Insurers normally consider trip length, age, medical conditions, cancellation value, policy excess, winter-sports cover and whether you want single-trip or annual insurance. Quotes for the same holiday can differ in both price and protection.

Paris city breaks: insure what you cannot refund

Paris trips often rely on a mixture of Eurostar or flights, hotels, timed museum entries and event tickets. List what you would lose if you had to cancel for a covered reason; a policy's cancellation limit should be compared with that sum, not simply the total advertised cost of the holiday.

If you travel with only hand luggage, baggage cover may be less important than medical assistance or cancellation. If you carry valuables, read the single-item cap and unattended-baggage exclusions.

Eurostar, French trains and missed connections

Insurance for a flight delay and protection for a train journey are not necessarily worded the same way. A train strike, late Eurostar arrival or missed separately booked TGV connection may fall outside some policies. Check definitions for common-carrier delay, missed departure, abandonment and additional accommodation.

Keep booking confirmations, operator notices and receipts. First check your rights with the transport operator: a travel policy may pay only for eligible losses that the carrier does not reimburse.

French Alps skiing and mountain rescue

A standard policy may exclude skiing, snowboarding, off-piste activity or mountain rescue unless a suitable extension is included. GHIC does not provide comprehensive rescue or repatriation protection. Check whether winter-sports equipment and piste closures matter for your trip, but prioritise emergency assistance and evacuation terms.

Declare the activities you intend to do and read the policy definition of guided versus unguided off-piste skiing. If there is no suitable option, do not assume a generic Europe policy will cover the activity.

Driving to France by car or campervan

Driving from Britain through the Channel Tunnel or by ferry adds vehicle-related risks. Medical travel insurance and breakdown cover serve different purposes; a travel policy normally does not replace your motor insurer's European driving cover. Check vehicle documents, breakdown assistance, roadside repatriation and separate-transport cancellation terms.

Over 65 or travelling with an existing condition?

Age alone does not determine cover, but some products set eligibility or trip-duration limits. If the insurer asks about medical history, answer fully. A policy that appears cheap can be unsuitable if it excludes a condition relevant to an emergency abroad.

France travel insurance: frequently asked questions

Does a UK GHIC replace travel insurance in France?

No. GHIC supports qualifying state healthcare under local rules. It does not replace insurance for repatriation, private care or cancellation.

Does insurance cover Eurostar delays?

It depends on the contract, cause, waiting periods and whether your loss is recoverable from the carrier. Read transport disruption wording carefully.

Is skiing automatically covered in France?

Not necessarily. Check the applicable winter-sports extension and exclusions before booking.
