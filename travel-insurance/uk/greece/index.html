Quick answer: what travel insurance matters for Greece from the UK?

UK travellers should compare medical and repatriation cover, ferry or flight delays, missed connections and the activities planned for Greek islands. An eligible UK GHIC or EHIC helps with medically necessary state healthcare under local rules; it does not replace travel insurance for repatriation or private clinics.

GHIC and healthcare in Greece for UK visitors

Carry a valid UK GHIC or eligible EHIC for medically necessary state healthcare in Greece, where accepted. Treatment is subject to Greek state-system rules and may involve fees. Check that the hospital or provider is part of the state system before assuming your card applies.

Private healthcare, medical repatriation and some treatment costs fall outside GHIC or EHIC. On smaller islands, reaching appropriate medical facilities can involve specialist transport. Ask any travel insurer what its emergency assistance line arranges, whether approval is needed before transfer and which providers are eligible.

Why Greek island travel needs extra attention

An Athens-only visit and an island-hopping holiday have different risks. A trip combining Athens, Santorini, Naxos and Crete may include flights, ferries and several independently booked hotels. Missing one ferry can affect a later leg even when the missed departure is not itself an insured event.

Will insurance cover a cancelled ferry in Greece?

A ferry cancellation due to weather or another disruption is not automatically a payable insurance claim. Policies define covered causes, minimum delay, missed-departure conditions and what documentation is required. Operators may have their own refund or rebooking obligations.

Save the ferry booking, cancellation notice, alternative travel receipts and hotel invoices. If the itinerary includes separate tickets, check whether the insurer's definition of a connection includes the exact legs you booked.

Medical evacuation and island emergencies

The nearest facility may not offer the specialist treatment required. Insurance can provide assistance coordinating transfers, but eligibility, pre-authorisation and geographic limits matter. Do not assume that a policy labelled “Europe” automatically pays for all helicopter, sea or air transfers.

In an emergency, use local emergency services first. Contact the insurer's assistance team as soon as practical, record the claim reference and keep all medical records, discharge summaries and receipts.

Scooters, quad bikes, sailing and diving

Scooter or ATV rental is common on Greek islands, yet insurers may exclude accidents related to vehicle type, licence entitlement, helmet use or local law. The rental contract and medical travel policy cover different liabilities. Check both before renting.

Likewise, recreational scuba diving, sailing and some excursions may need optional activity protection or have depth, supervision or equipment limits. Never rely on a generic sports checkbox without reading the activity definitions.

Over 65, families and pre-existing conditions

Some insurers restrict eligibility by age, prior diagnoses or journey length. Families should check all travellers are named or otherwise eligible under the policy and how emergency return for dependants is handled.

For senior travellers, read travel insurance over 65. If anyone has a relevant diagnosis, start with our guide to pre-existing conditions and complete any insurer medical screening accurately.

Before you buy: the Greece checklist

  1. Confirm the policy can be purchased by UK residents and Greece falls in the covered territory.
  2. Review medical expense limits, transfers, repatriation and assistance contact details.
  3. Check ferries, separate bookings, missed connections and delay waiting periods.
  4. Declare scooters, ATVs, diving or boat activities and check exclusions.
  5. Compare the real final quote, excess and non-refundable costs with the policy document.

Greece travel insurance: frequently asked questions

Can I use GHIC in Greece?

An eligible card supports medically necessary Greek state healthcare under local rules but does not replace travel insurance or pay for repatriation.

Are cancelled ferries insured?

Only when the policy covers the cause and the loss meets its terms. Check refund rights and keep written evidence.

Is a scooter accident automatically covered?

No. Licensing, engine size, protective equipment and exclusions can affect cover.
