Cities, islands and long rail days

Travel insurance for Spain from the USA

A Spain itinerary can shift quickly between major cities, coastal resorts and islands. The right policy should follow the whole trip—not just the flight into Madrid or Barcelona.

Compare for Spain

What to compare for Spain

Medical cover

Check the emergency medical limit, deductible and whether the plan pays providers directly or reimburses you later.

Evacuation

Island stays, mountain routes and cruises make transportation benefits worth reading carefully.

Personal belongings

Review per-item limits for phones, cameras and luggage rather than relying on the headline baggage total.

Trip interruption

Useful when a covered event forces you to leave a multi-city itinerary early and rebook transport.

What makes this trip different

Spain has both public and private medical facilities. A U.S. visitor should not assume access will be free or that an American health card will be accepted.

If your route includes the Balearic or Canary Islands, confirm that inter-island transport and medically necessary evacuation are within the policy wording.

Pickpocketing is a practical baggage concern in busy transport and tourist areas. Coverage still depends on reasonable care, prompt reporting and documentation.

Match coverage to the itinerary

Barcelona + Madrid by rail

Prioritize delay, missed connection and interruption language that applies to independently booked segments.

Mallorca or Tenerife

Check emergency transport, water activities and any scooter or adventure-sport exclusions.

Camino de Santiago

Look for walking or hiking coverage at your planned altitude, plus treatment and transport away from urban centers.

A quick reality check

Travel insurance only covers events, costs and circumstances defined by the policy. Buy for the wording you need, disclose information accurately and keep documentation for every significant booking.

Frequently asked questions

Is travel insurance required for Americans visiting Spain?

It is generally a risk-management choice rather than a standard insurance requirement for a short visa-free U.S. tourist visit. Entry rules can change, so verify official requirements before departure.

Will Medicare cover me in Spain?

Original Medicare usually provides very limited coverage outside the United States. Some Medicare Advantage or Medigap arrangements may differ, so confirm directly with the plan.

Should I insure train tickets separately?

Usually you insure the prepaid, non-refundable total trip cost. Check how the policy treats rail tickets, tours and independently booked connections.
