About iTravelEurope

How iTravelEurope researches and compares travel insurance.

iTravelEurope is an English-language travel-insurance research and comparison site focused on travel to Europe. We publish market-specific guidance because residence, healthcare entitlements, visa status, trip type and provider eligibility can materially change what a traveler should compare.

Editorial standards

Our goal is to make insurance comparisons understandable without pretending that one policy is automatically “best” for everyone. We do not publish invented star ratings, fake review counts or unsupported claims that an insurer is the best in the market.

We separate general travel protection from formal Schengen visa-insurance requirements and distinguish public healthcare rights such as GHIC/EHIC or Medicare rules from private travel-insurance benefits.

How our comparison methodology works

We compare only providers or products for which we can identify a relevant route or traveler use case and verify enough information to describe the product responsibly. The comparison can consider residence eligibility, destination or geographic area, trip dates, medical and repatriation benefits, cancellation or disruption options, visa suitability where relevant, exclusions that materially affect the trip and the price information available to us.

“Best deal” is an editorial default order, not a market-wide award or mathematical score. It reflects the relevance of the visible option to the trip and the information we can compare on the page. “Cheapest” sorts the visible options by the estimated trip total calculated on iTravelEurope. Neither label guarantees that a policy is the best choice for an individual traveler.

We do not claim to compare every insurer in the market. A provider may be absent because we do not yet have a commercial relationship, reliable product information, suitable eligibility data or a practical way to send the traveler to the correct quote flow.

How we handle prices and currency estimates

Prices shown on iTravelEurope can be editorial estimates, not live insurer quotes. Where we use an estimated daily rate, the page states that it is an estimate. Currency conversions are approximate and can change with exchange rates.

Our price assumptions are checked when a comparison is materially updated, when a provider or partner supplies a relevant product change, or when we identify that a published assumption may no longer reflect the provider's current offer. The provider's live quote at the time of purchase always overrides an iTravelEurope estimate.

We show a review date on materially updated editorial or comparison pages so readers can see when the page was last checked.

How we verify coverage and high-stakes facts

For insurance benefits, we prefer current provider product information, policy wording, benefit summaries or partner-supplied eligibility information. We avoid turning a marketing headline into a broader coverage claim. If a benefit depends on the exact plan, residence, age, trip dates or exclusions, we say so and direct the traveler to the provider for the final terms.

For rules that can materially affect health, visas or public healthcare, we prioritize first-party and official sources such as government agencies, the European Union, Medicare, NHS, CDC, consulates and official provider documentation. Sources are linked on the relevant guide whenever they materially support the advice.

Who writes and reviews the content

Pages are written and reviewed by the iTravelEurope Editorial Team. We do not claim a physician, lawyer, insurance broker or other specialist review unless that reviewer is specifically named on the page. Where specialist expertise matters, we rely on and cite the appropriate primary source rather than inventing an expert credential.

Editorial updates are triggered by material rule changes, provider/product changes, new source information, Search Console evidence that a page is answering a different user question, or a scheduled review of an actively maintained comparison or guide.

How we choose markets and topics

We build narrow country-of-residence and trip-intent clusters rather than hundreds of thin programmatic pages. Current coverage includes UK, U.S. and Canadian Europe travel-insurance journeys plus selected Schengen visa and destination-specific guides. New markets or guides are added when there is a clear traveler need and the relevant rules or provider eligibility can be verified.

Commercial relationships and commissions

Some provider links are affiliate links. If a traveler follows one and completes an eligible purchase, iTravelEurope may earn a commission. The commercial relationship does not by itself make a provider a “best deal,” and the final insurance purchase is completed with the provider rather than with iTravelEurope.

Affiliate links are disclosed on the comparison page and use sponsored link attributes where appropriate. See our affiliate disclosure for more detail.

Corrections and limitations

Insurance products and public rules can change. If a provider's live quote, current policy wording or official government guidance differs from a summary on iTravelEurope, the current provider or official source prevails. Readers can contact us if they believe a material fact needs correction.

What we are not

iTravelEurope is not an insurance company and does not underwrite policies. Our content is educational and cannot confirm individual eligibility, a future claim decision, visa approval, medical suitability or legal outcomes.
