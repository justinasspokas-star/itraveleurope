About iTravelEurope

Specific guidance beats a worldwide list of insurance links.

iTravelEurope is an English-language travel-insurance research site focused on people traveling to Europe. We launch market by market because residence changes product eligibility, existing health coverage and visa requirements.

Editorial standards

We separate general travel protection from formal Schengen visa-insurance requirements. We do not publish invented star ratings, fake review counts or “best insurer” claims without a transparent methodology and sufficient evidence.

High-stakes facts are checked against first-party or official sources where possible, such as the EU Visa Code, consular instructions, Medicare and Government of Canada travel guidance. Pages show a review date when the factual requirements can change.

How we choose markets

We start with narrow country-of-residence clusters rather than hundreds of programmatic pages. The first launch markets are India for Schengen visa insurance, and the United States and Canada for broader Europe travel insurance. Additional markets should be added only when the content and provider eligibility can be verified.

Commercial relationships

iTravelEurope may earn commission from insurance providers or comparison partners in the future. Commercial relationships should never change the price paid by the traveler or be presented as independent evidence that a policy is better. See the affiliate disclosure for details.

What we are not

iTravelEurope is not an insurance company and does not underwrite policies. Our content is educational and cannot confirm individual eligibility, a future claim decision, visa approval, or the medical suitability of a product.
