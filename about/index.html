About iTravelEurope

Specific guidance beats a worldwide list of insurance links.

iTravelEurope is an English-language travel-insurance research site focused on people traveling to Europe. We launch market by market because residence changes product eligibility, existing health coverage and visa requirements.

Editorial standards

We separate general travel protection from formal Schengen visa-insurance requirements. We do not publish invented star ratings, fake review counts or “best insurer” claims without a transparent methodology and sufficient evidence.

High-stakes facts are checked against first-party or official sources where possible, such as the EU Visa Code, consular instructions, Medicare and Government of Canada travel guidance. Pages show a review date when the factual requirements can change.

How we choose markets

We start with narrow country-of-residence clusters rather than hundreds of programmatic pages. The first launch markets are India for Schengen visa insurance, and the United States and Canada for broader Europe travel insurance. Additional markets should be added only when the content and provider eligibility can be verified.

Commercial relationships

iTravelEurope may earn commission from insurance providers or comparison partners in the future. Commercial relationships should never change the price paid by the traveler or be presented as independent evidence that a policy is better. See the affiliate disclosure for details.

What we are not

iTravelEurope is not an insurance company and does not underwrite policies. Our content is educational and cannot confirm individual eligibility, a future claim decision, visa approval, or the medical suitability of a product.

Editorial methodology

How iTravelEurope researches and updates travel insurance content

Travel insurance, healthcare entitlements and visa rules can change. Our goal is to separate those three topics clearly and use the strongest available source for each claim.

Source hierarchy

  1. Law and official government sources for visa requirements, entry rules and regulatory obligations.
  2. Public healthcare authorities for Medicare, GHIC/EHIC and other health-entitlement information.
  3. Official insurer policy documents for product-specific benefits, exclusions and eligibility.
  4. Provider or assistance documentation for claims processes, certificates and operational details.
  5. Independent secondary sources only when a primary source is unavailable or when they add clearly identified context.

How pages are reviewed

Pages that depend on changing rules include a reviewed or modified date. When a primary source changes materially, the affected guidance should be updated rather than leaving an old recommendation in place. We do not treat an old article date as proof that a rule is still current.

How we handle comparisons

We compare eligibility and policy wording before price. A low premium is not useful if the insurer cannot sell to the traveler, if the territory does not cover the trip or if an important benefit is excluded. We do not publish provider rankings or star scores unless the methodology and underlying data can be explained and verified.

Medical and visa content

iTravelEurope provides general information, not individualized medical, legal or insurance advice. We explain public rules and policy concepts, then link to the relevant authority so travelers can verify the requirement that applies to their own documents, health situation and itinerary.

Corrections policy

If a reader, insurer, government body or other source identifies an error, we review the cited evidence and correct the page when appropriate. Material corrections should preserve the page's intent while updating the affected facts and reviewed date.

Commercial independence

Affiliate relationships can support the site, but they do not change the legal requirements, policy wording or editorial conclusions we publish. Commercial relationships are disclosed separately in our affiliate disclosure.

Contact the editorial team

For corrections, source suggestions or questions about our methodology, use the contact page. We prefer evidence that can be checked publicly or against an official policy document.
