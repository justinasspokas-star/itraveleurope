How we write

Useful first. Search-friendly second.

Our guides are built around the questions travelers actually need to answer, not around repeating a keyword. We separate public healthcare entitlements, visa requirements and insurance-policy benefits so the reader can verify each one.

  1. Use primary government and health-system sources for rules that can change.
  2. Explain where policy wording varies instead of pretending one answer fits every insurer.
  3. Use examples, tables and checklists to make complex decisions easier to verify.
  4. Link to the relevant country-of-residence and destination guides for the next step.
