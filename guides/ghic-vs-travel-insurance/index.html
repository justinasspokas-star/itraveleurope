GHIC vs travel insurance: they solve different problems

A GHIC can help with medically necessary state healthcare in covered countries. Travel insurance is designed to address a broader set of financial risks around the trip.

Reviewed October 3, 2026

What GHIC is for

The UK Global Health Insurance Card gives eligible UK residents access to medically necessary state healthcare during temporary stays in participating countries, under the same basis as people insured in that country. That can mean treatment is free or that a local patient contribution still applies.

Check the destination-specific rules before travel because coverage is tied to the public healthcare system and not every country or territory is identical.

What GHIC is not designed to cover

Medical repatriationReturning you to the UK after a serious medical event is a separate travel-insurance issue.
Private healthcareDo not assume treatment outside the state system is included.
CancellationGHIC does not protect prepaid flights, hotels or tours if a covered event forces cancellation.
Baggage & travel disruptionLost belongings, delays and missed departures are separate travel risks.

How to use both intelligently

Do not treat the choice as “GHIC or travel insurance.” For an eligible traveler, GHIC can be useful healthcare access while a travel policy can address the financial risks outside that system. When comparing policies, disclose information accurately and read how the insurer expects you to use GHIC or public treatment where available.

  • Carry a valid GHIC or eligible EHIC if you can use one.
  • Compare medical repatriation and emergency assistance.
  • Match cancellation cover to the money genuinely at risk.
  • Check activities, age limits and pre-existing-condition wording.

When the difference matters most

The gap becomes more important on expensive trips, cruises, ski holidays, long stays or itineraries with many prepaid bookings. A public healthcare card may help with treatment, but it cannot recover every financial loss created by an interrupted trip.
