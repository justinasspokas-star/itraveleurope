Does Medicare cover travel in Europe?

The short answer is that Original Medicare usually provides very limited coverage outside the United States. The useful answer is more specific: your exact combination of Medicare, Medigap, Medicare Advantage and employer or retiree benefits determines what happens next.

iTravelEurope EditorialReviewed September 24, 2026

Start with your exact plan—not the Medicare name

Original Medicare generally does not cover healthcare outside the United States except in narrow circumstances. That does not automatically tell you what a Medicare Advantage plan, Medigap policy or retiree plan will do.

Call the number on your plan card and ask for a written explanation of overseas emergency and urgent-care benefits. Ask whether you pay first, whether preauthorization is needed, which documents must be submitted and whether medical evacuation is included. Evacuation is often the largest gap because ordinary health coverage may pay for treatment without paying to transport you home.

Four questions to ask before comparing travel plans

First, what is the maximum overseas medical benefit? Second, is there a deductible or coinsurance? Third, does the plan arrange direct payment or only reimburse after you return? Fourth, is emergency medical evacuation covered and who decides where you are transported?

Write down the answers. When you compare travel insurance, you can then buy around the gaps instead of duplicating benefits blindly. A traveler with meaningful overseas benefits may prioritize cancellation and evacuation; someone with no overseas medical protection may need a stronger medical limit.

Why pre-existing-condition wording matters

Travel insurance does not use one universal definition. Some plans offer a waiver of the pre-existing-condition exclusion when the policy is purchased within a stated time after the initial trip deposit and other conditions are met. The waiver is not the same as covering routine care or a planned treatment abroad.

List recent diagnoses, medication changes, tests and treatment, then read the look-back period and stability rules. If the wording is unclear, ask the licensed provider before buying and keep the response.

A practical decision

Do not choose solely from a price table. Match medical coverage, evacuation, trip cost and your health history to the itinerary. A two-week city trip, a Mediterranean cruise and a remote hiking holiday do not present the same transport or cancellation risk.
