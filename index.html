Useful before a quote

Five questions that matter more than a cheap premium.

A useful comparison starts with your actual risk, not a generic “Europe” label.

  1. Can this insurer sell to residents of my country? Eligibility comes before price.
  2. What happens in a medical emergency? Check limits, deductible, payment process and evacuation rules.
  3. What prepaid money is genuinely at risk? Cancellation cover should match non-refundable bookings.
  4. What does the policy exclude? Read pre-existing-condition, activity and alcohol-related wording.
  5. Do I need a visa-compliant certificate? Visa insurance and ordinary travel protection are different needs.
